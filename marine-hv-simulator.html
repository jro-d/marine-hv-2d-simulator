Marine High Voltage – Step-by-Step Tutorial Guide

Mission 1: Bow Thruster 6.6 kV Motor Isolation & PTW
Follow each step below. You can click "Do This Step" to execute it immediately or click the highlighted controls on screen.
UK MCA COSWP Chapter 22 & STCW 2010 Manila Amendments Table A-III/6:
Under international maritime law, any work on marine high voltage (> 1,000 V) equipment must strictly adhere to the 5 Golden Rules in chronological order.
1. Disconnect Completely (All-Pole Isolation)

Isolate the apparatus from all sources of electrical supply. This includes racking out the primary Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) to the DISCONNECTED position and isolating all low-voltage auxiliary and transformer secondary circuits that could back-feed into the apparatus.

2. Secure Against Re-Connection (LOTO & Interlocks)

Apply personal safety padlocks to the VCB racking shutter port, lock and extract Castell trapped keys (transferring them to earth switches), lock all keys inside the designated Safety Key Box, and post "DANGER — DO NOT OPERATE (MEN WORKING)" warning notices.

3. Verify That the Installation is Dead (Prove – Test – Prove)

Never assume a circuit is dead because a breaker was opened! You must test all three phases (L1, L2, L3) using an approved 6.6 kV live-line voltage detector wand. The wand MUST be proven on a dedicated electronic proving unit immediately before and immediately after testing the terminals.

4. Carry Out Earthing and Short-Circuiting

Close the Integral Fault-Making Earth Switch and/or apply Portable Circuit Main Earth (CME) leads. Earthing bleeds any residual capacitive stored charge and ensures that if a circuit is accidentally energized, upstream protection instantly trips before lethal voltages can develop.

5. Provide Protection Against Adjacent Live Parts & Issue Permit (PTW)

Padlock automatic metallic safety shutters covering adjacent live busbars, erect safety barriers, and formally issue a signed High Voltage Permit-to-Work (PTW) or Sanction-for-Test (SFT) before permitting any physical work to begin.

Mission 01: Bow Thruster 6.6 kV Motor Isolation & PTW

Objective: Isolate the 1,800 kW Bow Thruster motor feeder (BT-M1) to replace the drive-end bearing.
Key Steps: Stop drive -> Equip PPE & roll test gloves -> Trip VCB [O] -> Rack to DISCONNECTED (0/20) -> Extract K1-BT -> Padlock racking port & post Danger Tag -> Insert K1-BT into Earth Switch -> Close & padlock Earth Switch -> Lock Safety Key Box -> Open door -> Padlock Red Busbar Shutter -> Prove–Test–Prove -> Issue PTW.

Mission 02: Transformer Dual-End Isolation (The Back-Feed Trap)

Objective: Isolate HV/LV Transformer TR1 (6.6 kV / 440 V) while the 440 V switchboard remains energized from an auxiliary generator.
The Trap: If you only trip the 6.6 kV primary, 440 V back-feeds into the secondary winding, stepping up to lethal 6,600 V on the HV terminals! You must isolate & lock out ACB-TR1-LV first and exchange dual Castell keys (K-HV + K-LV).

Mission 03: Sanction-for-Test & 5 kV Megger / PI Test

Objective: Perform a 5,000 V DC Insulation Resistance & Polarization Index test on the isolated motor feeder.
Key Steps: Cancel active PTW -> Issue Sanction-for-Test (SFT) -> Open Integral Earth Switch (cannot megger an earthed winding) -> Connect leads & run 5 kV test (PI = 2.49 PASS) -> CRITICAL: Bleed 4,850 V DC stored capacitive charge down to 0 V before disconnecting leads -> Re-close Earth Switch -> Cancel SFT.

Mission 04: De-Isolation & Safe Restoration to Service

Objective: Cancel permit and return the Bow Thruster feeder to service.
Key Steps: Sign off PTW return -> Remove Portable Earths (L1, L2, L3 phase clamps FIRST via Hot-Stick, PE ship earth clamp LAST) -> Close & bolt cubicle door -> Unlock Key Box & open Earth Switch -> Return K1-BT to VCB -> Rack to TEST (10/20) & perform Close/Trip check -> Rack fully to SERVICE (20/20).

Q: Why did I receive an automatic safety deduction for donning gloves?
Under STCW Table A-III/6, you must roll up the gauntlet of Class 2 insulating gloves to perform a pneumatic air-leak test for pinholes immediately before donning. Failing to air-test is an automatic failure.
Q: Why is the racking crank handle blocked from turning?
Under IEC 60092-503 mechanical interlock rules, the racking shutter flap refuses to open if the breaker is CLOSED [I] or if the racking port has a padlock attached. Press TRIP [O] first!
Q: Why is Castell Key K1-BT trapped in the breaker?
Castell keys are mechanically trapped whenever the VCB is in SERVICE (20 turns) or TEST (10 turns). The key will only rotate and release when the truck reaches DISCONNECTED (0 turns).
Q: Why can't I close the Integral Earth Switch?
The Earth Switch is locked until the VCB is completely in DISCONNECTED (0 turns) AND the matching Castell key has been transferred and turned in the earth switch lock cylinder. On Transformer TR1, BOTH K-HV and K-LV must be inserted!
Q: Why did my 5 kV Megger read 0.0 MΩ in Mission 3?
You cannot Megger an earthed circuit! The Integral Earth Switch was still closed, shorting the 5,000 V test voltage directly to the ship's hull. Unlock and open the Earth Switch first under an active Sanction-for-Test.
Q: What is the Reverse CME Removal Rule in Mission 4?
When removing Portable Earths, always unclamp phase clamps (L1, L2, L3) via Hot-Stick FIRST, and disconnect the PE Earth Bar clamp LAST. Disconnecting PE first leaves a floating conductor at lethal induced voltage in your hands!

The 5 Golden Rules of Marine HV Safety:
  1. Disconnect completely (All-pole isolation from all sources, including 440 V back-feed).
  2. Secure against re-connection (LOTO padlocks, shutter locks, Castell trapped keys).
  3. Verify dead (Prove – Test – Prove using rated 6.6 kV wand & proving unit).
  4. Carry out earthing and short-circuiting (Integral earth switch and/or portable CME leads).
  5. Provide protection against adjacent live parts & issue formal Permit-to-Work (PTW) or SFT.
